\opening{Dear Editor,}

We are pleased to submit our manuscript entitled \textbf{``Regulatory Alignment Agent: Multi-Step Retrieval with Domain-Aware Query Reformulation for Automated Compliance Mapping''} for consideration for publication in \textit{Data \& Knowledge Engineering}.

This paper introduces the Regulatory Alignment Agent (RAA), a multi-step retrieval system that automates compliance mapping---the task of linking regulatory requirements to organizational controls. The RAA employs a tool-augmented reasoning loop inspired by the ReAct paradigm, combining multi-backend retrieval with Reciprocal Rank Fusion, domain-aware query reformulation via a curated compliance thesaurus, cross-framework corroboration, bidirectional verification, and calibrated selective prediction.

We believe this work is well suited to DKE for the following reasons:

\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Knowledge representation and retrieval:} The paper addresses how domain-specific knowledge (regulatory terminology, framework relationships, compliance concepts) can be systematically encoded and leveraged to improve information retrieval in specialized domains.

    \item \textbf{System architecture:} The multi-step agent architecture, with its tool-augmented reasoning loop, presents a novel system design pattern for knowledge-intensive retrieval tasks that require auditability.

    \item \textbf{Rigorous evaluation:} We present a controlled ablation study across 30 random seeds with statistical significance testing, demonstrating that domain-aware query reformulation is the dominant contributor ($\Delta$Top-1 = +0.116, $p < 0.001$).
\end{enumerate}

The manuscript has not been published previously and is not under consideration elsewhere. All authors have approved the manuscript and agree with its submission to \textit{Data \& Knowledge Engineering}.

The benchmark, evaluation harness, and agent implementation are publicly available at \url{https://github.com/sumanshusohal/RAA-Compliance-Mapping}.

Thank you for considering our submission. We look forward to your response.

\closing{Sincerely,}
